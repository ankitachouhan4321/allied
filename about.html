About Us
21+ Years of Excellence in Bathroom Fittings

With over 21 years of excellence, Allied has established itself as a trusted name in designing and manufacturing premium bath fittings aligned with global plumbing standards. Our advanced infrastructure, combined with cutting-edge technology and a highly skilled workforce, enables us to deliver products that reflect precision, durability, and superior quality.

Every Allied product undergoes rigorous quality checks at every stage of manufacturing, ensuring unmatched performance and reliability. Our commitment to innovation and excellence has been the foundation of our continued growth and customer trust.

As the concept of bathrooms evolves into sophisticated lifestyle spaces, Allied has embraced this transformation with vision and expertise. In a short span, we have expanded our presence globally, introducing a range of bath solutions that redefine elegance and functionality.

Our thoughtfully crafted collections are designed to be enduring, exquisite, and visually striking—bringing a perfect balance of style and performance. Backed by a dedicated team of professionals, Allied continues to innovate and deliver inspiring bathroom solutions for modern living.
